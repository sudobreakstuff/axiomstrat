What is AxiomStrat?

It's a full strategy laboratory that runs on your PC. You design a trading strategy in a visual builder — RSI reversals, EMA trends, grids, mean reversion, or your own Python logic — and AxiomStrat does the hard part: it proves whether the strategy actually works.

Every backtest is replay-verified. The engine records every trade, and an independent checker replays each one from the raw price data — entry, partial take-profits, stop-losses, commissions, slippage and spread — and matches the equity curve to the cent. The 62-check release harness is rerun against real MT5 history before a build is published.

Then Monte Carlo and train/test splits measure whether your edge is real or curve-fitted — and if it survives, you deploy it to MT5 in one click, with drawdown limits, daily trade caps, session windows and an economic-event guard baked in. Because your PC runs the show, you can pair a browser on the same trusted network to monitor deployments and send start, stop or emergency-flatten commands.

Why I built it

I got tired of paying for backtest tools that couldn't explain their own numbers — and of strategy marketplaces where "98% win rate" meant "screenshot of a spreadsheet". So I built my own, and I made it honest: the engine audits itself, every trade is replayable, and the verification harness is part of the release process.

AxiomStrat is a business, not a hobby. That means it has to prove itself every single day — your results, auditable to the cent; your data, kept on your machine; and access controlled by a signed license key.
